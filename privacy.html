ECH Business Hub Privacy Notice

Effective September 15, 2026

The ECH Business Hub is an internal business tool operated for The Electronics Clearing House LLC. It connects to the company's eBay seller account so authorized staff can prepare inventory offers and publish them only after Lee's separate approval.

Information used

The connection may access the eBay seller username, inventory and offer information, account policies, and inventory-location identifiers needed to prepare and publish approved listings. Listing records may include product details, prices, quantities, condition, photos, and eBay listing identifiers.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the internal review-and-publication workflow, verify seller configuration, create or update inventory offers, and keep an audit record of approvals and submitted values. Paige's submission cannot publish a listing; publication requires Lee's authenticated approval.

Storage and sharing

eBay access credentials are kept on a secured server and are not placed in Listing Studio or sent to staff browsers. Information is not sold. It is shared only with service providers required to operate the workflow, including eBay and the secured hosting and database services used by the Business Hub.

Retention and access

Connection records and listing audit records are retained for legitimate business, security, and accounting needs. Access is limited to authorized company users. The eBay connection can be revoked through the seller's eBay account or by an authorized Business Hub administrator.

Contact

Questions about this tool or its data handling can be sent through The Install Spot contact page.
